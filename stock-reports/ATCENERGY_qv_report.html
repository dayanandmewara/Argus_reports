Business Overview

Incorporated 2020 · NSE SME listed 2 Apr 2025 · Face value ₹10

ATC Energies System Ltd designs, assembles and exports lithium-ion batteries out of factories in Vasai, Thane and Noida (NCR), with a combined manufacturing/testing footprint of roughly 3,160 sq. mt. The product range spans mini-batteries for the banking sector (ATM/POS backup), EV and two/three/four-wheeler packs, energy storage systems, UPS/inverter cells, solar and agricultural equipment batteries, and drone batteries. Chairman and CEO Sandeep Gangabishan Bajoria is a first-generation entrepreneur; the company describes itself as being among Asia's top-10 lithium battery brands, though this is a company self-characterisation rather than an independently verified ranking.

The company raised ₹63.76 Cr via its March 2025 SME IPO (price band ₹112–118) primarily to repay/prepay borrowings on its Noida factory and fund capex/working capital there. The stock listed at a 9.32% discount to issue price and has fallen further since — down roughly 75–77% from its 29-Apr-2025 all-time high of ₹115.35 to the current ₹26.4 (6 Aug 2026).

Why This Stock — Series Rationale

This ticket is included in the series precisely because it stress-tests the BQV v5.0 framework's guardrails. ATC Energies looked, on paper, like a scarce India-listed pure-play on lithium battery localisation at IPO. Fourteen months later, FY26 (year to Mar 2026) revenue fell 40% YoY, operating margin collapsed from ~32% to ~7% (and turned negative in H2), net profit swung to a loss, and operating cash flow went to −₹32.8 Cr against a full-year revenue base of only ₹30.7 Cr — a cash outflow larger than the company's entire top line. Combined with heavy revenue concentration in two promoter-group entities, this is a textbook case for the O'Glove earnings-quality hard gate blocking entry regardless of how statistically cheap the stock now looks on P/B.
