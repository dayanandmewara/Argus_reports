Dhoot designs, engineers, manufactures and supplies wiring harnesses and electrical/electronic distribution systems for both ICE and EV platforms — main/dash/floor/battery harnesses, high-voltage interconnects, battery packs, automotive switches, sensors, electronic controllers, connectors, terminals and power cords. Customers span two-wheelers, three-wheelers, commercial vehicles, off-highway/farm equipment: Bajaj Auto, TVS Motor, Hero MotoCorp, Honda Motorcycle & Scooter India, Royal Enfield, KTM, Suzuki. Wiring harnesses are 77.1% of FY26 revenue; the balance — battery packs, sensors/controllers, switches — doubled in two years to ₹1,037 Cr FY26. EV-linked revenue rose from 16.19% of revenue (FY24) to 24.18% (FY26) VERIFIED. Promoters: BC Asia Investments XV Ltd (Bain Capital vehicle) and founder-MD Rahul Radhavallabh Dhoot.

Why This Series Entry Is Interesting — and Challenging

The Structural Case

An EV two-wheeler needs 1.5–2.5x the harness content of a petrol one (HV cables, battery interconnects, charger links). Dhoot already owns ~70% of that specific segment — pure volume-agnostic content-per-vehicle upside as India's 2W EV penetration triples over five years.

The Price Problem

Listed at 37.8% premium to an already-rich 44.9x trailing P/E, while EBITDA and PAT margins have compressed every year since FY24 and free cash flow has been thin-to-negative through a heavy capex cycle. Good business; the entry price on Day 1 does most of the framework's work for you.
